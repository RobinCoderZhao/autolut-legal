HUEO Support

Local Log video restoration and color processing for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with tools for creating Live Photos and importing external-device media into Photos.

Get help

Import and processing

Select videos through the system Photos or Files picker. The App creates managed working copies and keeps the selected source unchanged.

Import to Photos

Choose an external-device directory, preview supported photos, videos, and Live Photos, then select the items to add to Photos. Source files stay unchanged. Keep the device connected until the import finishes.

Video to Live Photo

Choose a video, prepare a Live Photo, and save it to Photos. The video picker uses the Photos access you grant. Available source capture metadata is used where supported; missing metadata cannot be reconstructed.

Export allowance

Free users receive 30 successful full-quality exports. Failed, cancelled, and preview-only renders do not consume the allowance.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases on the Pro screen for eligible subscriptions or Lifetime Pro purchases made with your Apple Account.

Subscriptions

Subscription billing and cancellation are managed by Apple. Buying Lifetime Pro does not automatically cancel an existing subscription.

Report a problem

Open Settings > Diagnostics and choose Prepare Diagnostic Package, then Share or Save Diagnostic Package if you want to provide troubleshooting details. Preparing the ZIP does not upload it. The package excludes source media, thumbnails, media filenames, paths, capture times, locations, and Photos identifiers. You can use Delete Local Diagnostics without deleting your media or import history.

Contact

When contacting support, include the App version, device model, operating-system version, and a description of what happened. You may attach a diagnostic package if useful. Share source media only when needed and when you have the right to do so; check for private content and embedded location first.

原画光年支持

适用于 iPhone、iPad 和 Mac 的本地 Log 视频还原与色彩处理工具，并提供视频转 Live Photo 和外接设备媒体导入相册功能。

获取帮助

导入与处理

请通过系统“照片”或“文件”选择器选择视频。本应用会创建受管理的工作副本，并保持所选源文件不变。

导入相册

选择外接设备目录，预览受支持的照片、视频和 Live Photo，再选择要写入系统相册的项目。源文件保持不变；导入完成前请保持设备连接。

视频转 Live Photo

选择视频，准备 Live Photo 并保存到系统相册。视频选择页面使用您授予的照片读取权限；在支持的范围内使用源媒体已有的拍摄元数据，无法重建缺失的信息。

免费导出额度

免费用户可成功导出 30 个完整质量文件。失败、取消和仅用于预览的渲染不会消耗额度。

恢复购买

如需恢复使用同一 Apple 账户购买的有效订阅或永久 Pro，请在 Pro 页面选择“恢复购买”。

订阅管理

订阅的扣款与取消由 Apple 管理。购买永久 Pro 不会自动取消已有订阅。

反馈问题

打开“设置 > 诊断”，选择“准备诊断包”，再按需选择“分享或保存诊断包”，即可提供排查信息。准备 ZIP 不会自动上传。诊断包不包含源媒体、缩略图、媒体文件名、路径、拍摄时间、位置或 Photos 标识。您可以“删除本地诊断”，不会删除媒体或导入历史。
