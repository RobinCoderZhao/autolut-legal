Import and processing

Select videos through the system Photos or Files picker. The App creates managed working copies and keeps the selected source unchanged.

Export allowance

Free users receive 30 successful full-quality exports. Failed, cancelled, and preview-only renders do not consume the allowance.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases on the Pro screen for eligible subscriptions or Lifetime Pro purchases made with your Apple Account.

Subscriptions

Subscription billing and cancellation are managed by Apple. Buying Lifetime Pro does not automatically cancel an existing subscription.
