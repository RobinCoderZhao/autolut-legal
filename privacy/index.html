1. Introduction

HUEO (the “App,” Chinese name “原画光年”) is an iOS, iPadOS, and macOS application developed by Robin (“we,” “us,” or “our”). It provides Log-video restoration and color processing, video-to-Live-Photo creation, and import of supported external-device media into Photos. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use the App.

Privacy summary: the App is local-first. We do not operate an account, media, analytics, or quota server. The App does not automatically send us your media, LUTs, editing recipes, or diagnostics. We do not receive payment credentials. You decide whether to share a diagnostic package or other information when contacting support.

3. Photos and Files access

Photos

You choose videos through Apple's system photo picker. To preserve original media quality and metadata, the App may request Photo Library read/write permission and use the identifier of the item you selected to resolve its original video resource through Apple's Photos framework. The video-to-Live-Photo picker also uses Photos read access to display the videos, thumbnails, duration, dimensions, and capture dates available within your authorization. With limited access, only the items permitted by the system are available. Original video resources are imported for processing after you select them; the App does not upload your library to us.

When browsing or selecting media stored in iCloud Photos, Apple may download thumbnails or the selected original resource to your device. That transfer is provided by Apple and is governed by your Apple account, device settings, and Apple's privacy terms.

Saving an exported video, a generated Live Photo, or selected external-device media requests Photos add permission. External-device import does not request full-library read access for duplicate checking. Video-to-Live-Photo recovery may check a previously created item if full read access was already granted.

Files

You may select videos and LUTs through Apple's system Files picker. The App accesses the selected items within the access granted by the operating system.

External devices and capture metadata

For Import to Photos, you authorize a device or directory through the system directory picker. The App searches for supported photos, videos, and Live Photo resources within that directory and its permitted subdirectories. It does not search outside that scope or modify, move, rename, or delete the source files. Only the items you select and start importing are added to Photos.

Available capture time, embedded location, and other media metadata may be read locally and retained in supported imported or generated media. This reads existing media metadata; the App does not request the device's current location or record GPS tracks. It cannot reconstruct information missing from the source. Review media metadata before sharing an output that may contain capture time or location.

4. Local storage and retention

  • Working copies: imported media is copied into App-managed storage so the selected source can remain unchanged. Working copies may remain while the current task is active or recoverable.
  • Temporary files: previews, candidate renders, and delivery files are stored temporarily and removed after success, failure, cancellation, or recovery cleanup, subject to normal operating-system behavior.
  • Presets and LUTs: user-created presets and imported LUTs remain until you delete them or remove the App's local data.
  • Workspace data: completing or replacing a session removes related managed work files when they are no longer needed. Uninstalling the App removes its local container, subject to Apple's platform behavior.
  • External-device import: authorization bookmarks, local import fingerprints, and recovery records remain in App storage to support reconnection and interrupted imports. Thumbnail disk caching has a 200 MiB limit. Temporary import copies, extracted Live Photo resources, and preview clips are cleaned up after completion, failure, cancellation, or a later launch.
  • Saved results: delivered videos, photos, and Live Photos remain in Photos or your chosen destination until you delete them. If iCloud Photos is enabled, Apple may synchronize items saved to Photos under your system settings.

Local diagnostics and optional sharing

The App keeps a bounded local record of processing stages, session and incident times, random session identifiers, hashed item tokens, resource pressure, counts, and error codes. Apple MetricKit reports are filtered before storage to retain numeric performance and crash information plus App/build, operating-system, architecture, and binary details needed for troubleshooting. Other free text is removed.

Diagnostic records and packages exclude media, thumbnails, user media filenames and paths, capture times, locations, camera/lens strings, Photos identifiers, and access bookmarks. Import-recovery information included in a package is reduced to batch phases, state/error counts, and recovery-status information.

Incident cleanup applies limits of 20 records, 30 days, and 50 MiB. Diagnostic storage is excluded from system backup. Diagnostics are not automatically uploaded. In Settings > Diagnostics, you can prepare a ZIP and then choose whether to share or save it using the system share sheet. Temporary diagnostic ZIPs older than 24 hours are cleaned up when the App starts or you prepare another package; the system may remove temporary files earlier.

Delete Local Diagnostics removes the App's local diagnostic records and temporary packages without deleting media or import history/recovery records. Copies you have already saved or sent outside the App must be managed separately. If you contact support, we receive the contact details, message, and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond and investigate the issue.

5. Free-export allowance and iCloud Keychain

Free users currently receive 30 successful full-quality exports. To record delivered exports, the App stores a minimal, append-only event in the system Keychain. Each event contains a random export-job identifier, policy/schema information, an App-family identifier, and commit time. It does not contain the filename, file path, Photos asset identifier, video metadata, camera profile, LUT, editing recipe, or media content.

If iCloud Keychain is enabled, Apple may eventually synchronize these Keychain items among your eligible iPhone, iPad, and Mac devices signed in to the same Apple account. The App does not actively contact an account or quota server, cannot read your Apple ID, and cannot force or guarantee when Apple completes synchronization. Devices can therefore show temporarily different remaining counts while offline or before Keychain convergence. Reinstalling the App does not guarantee that the allowance resets.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes payment credentials, billing, storefront pricing, taxes, subscription management, refunds, and purchase history under Apple's terms and privacy policy. The App receives verified product and entitlement information needed to display products and grant Pro access. We do not receive your card number or Apple account password.

The App uses Apple's sandbox, system permission controls, StoreKit verification, and Keychain protection. No method of storage is completely secure, so you should keep important source media backed up and protect access to your devices and Apple account.

11. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not require a developer-operated account. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated service.

3. 照片与文件访问

照片

您通过 Apple 的系统照片选择器选择视频。为了保留原始媒体质量和元数据，本应用可能请求照片图库读写权限，并使用您所选项目的标识，通过 Apple Photos 框架解析该项目的原始视频资源。“视频转 Live Photo”的选择页面也会使用照片读取权限，在您的授权范围内展示视频、缩略图、时长、尺寸和拍摄日期。使用有限访问权限时，只能访问系统允许的项目。您选定视频后，应用才导入原始视频资源进行处理；不会向我们上传您的照片图库。

浏览或选择存储在 iCloud 照片中的媒体时，Apple 可能将缩略图或所选原始资源下载到您的设备。该传输由 Apple 提供，并受您的 Apple 账户、设备设置和 Apple 隐私条款约束。

保存导出视频、生成的 Live Photo 或所选外接设备媒体时，会请求照片添加权限。外接设备导入不会为了去重请求完整图库读取权限。视频转 Live Photo 的恢复流程仅在已获得完整读取权限时，才可能核对先前创建的项目。

文件

您可以通过 Apple 的系统文件选择器选择视频和 LUT。本应用在操作系统授予的访问范围内读取这些项目。

外接设备与拍摄元数据

使用“导入相册”时，您通过系统目录选择器授权设备或目录。本应用只在该目录及允许访问的子目录内查找受支持的照片、视频和 Live Photo 资源，不会搜索授权范围外的目录，也不会修改、移动、重命名或删除源文件。只有您选定并开始导入的项目才会写入系统相册。

可用的拍摄时间、媒体内嵌位置及其他元数据可能在本地读取，并保留到受支持的导入或生成结果中。这是读取已有媒体元数据；本应用不会请求设备当前位置或记录 GPS 轨迹，也无法重建源文件缺失的信息。分享可能带有拍摄时间或位置的结果前，请检查其元数据。

4. 本地存储与保留

  • 工作副本：导入的媒体会复制到应用管理的存储中，使所选源文件保持不变。当前任务处于活动或可恢复状态时，工作副本可能继续保留。
  • 临时文件：预览、候选渲染和交付文件会临时保存，并在成功、失败、取消或恢复清理后删除，但仍受操作系统正常行为影响。
  • 预设与 LUT：用户创建的预设和导入的 LUT 会保留到您主动删除或移除本应用本地数据。
  • 工作区数据：完成或替换会话后，本应用会在不再需要时清理相关工作文件。卸载应用会按照 Apple 平台行为移除本地应用容器。
  • 外接设备导入：授权书签、本机导入指纹和恢复记录保存在应用存储中，用于重新连接和恢复中断的导入。缩略图磁盘缓存上限为 200 MiB。导入临时副本、拆分的 Live Photo 资源和预览片段，会在完成、失败、取消或后续启动时清理。
  • 保存结果：成功交付的视频、照片和 Live Photo 会保留在系统相册或您选择的位置，直至您删除。启用 iCloud 照片时，Apple 可能根据系统设置同步已保存到相册的项目。

本地诊断与自愿分享

本应用在本地保存容量受限的处理阶段记录、会话和异常时间、随机会话标识、散列项目标记、资源压力、计数和错误码。Apple MetricKit 报告会在保存前筛选，仅保留数值性能与崩溃信息，以及排查所需的应用版本、构建号、操作系统、架构和二进制程序信息；其他自由文本会被移除。

诊断记录及诊断包不包含媒体、缩略图、用户媒体文件名及路径、拍摄时间、位置、相机或镜头字符串、Photos 标识或授权书签。诊断包中的导入恢复信息只保留批次阶段、状态和错误计数，以及恢复状态信息。

异常记录按 20 条、30 天和 50 MiB 的上限执行清理，并排除系统备份。诊断不会自动上传。您可以在“设置 > 诊断”中准备 ZIP，然后通过系统分享面板自行决定是否分享或保存。超过 24 小时的临时诊断 ZIP 会在应用启动或再次准备诊断包时清理；系统也可能提前回收临时文件。

“删除本地诊断”会删除应用内的诊断记录和临时诊断包，不会删除媒体、导入历史或导入恢复记录。已经另存或发送到应用之外的副本需由您另行管理。联系支持时，我们会收到您主动发送的联系方式、消息和附件，并用于回复及排查该问题。
