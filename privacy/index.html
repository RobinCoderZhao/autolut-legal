1. Introduction

Original Image Lightyear (the “App,” Chinese name “原画光年”) is an iOS, iPadOS, and macOS Log-video restoration and color-processing application developed by Robin (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use the App.

Privacy summary: the App is local-first. We do not operate an account, media, analytics, or quota server. We do not receive your videos, LUTs, editing recipes, or payment credentials.

3. Photos and Files access

Photos

You choose videos through Apple's system photo picker. To preserve original media quality and metadata, the App may request Photo Library read/write permission and use the identifier of the item you selected to resolve its original video resource through Apple's Photos framework. The App does not use that permission to browse or upload unselected media.

If the selected original exists only in iCloud Photos, Apple may download it to your device. That transfer is provided by Apple and is governed by your Apple account, device settings, and Apple's privacy terms.

Files

You may select videos and LUTs through Apple's system Files picker. The App accesses only the items you select and any security-scoped access granted by the operating system for those items.

4. Local storage and retention

  • Working copies: imported media is copied into App-managed storage so the selected source can remain unchanged. Working copies may remain while the current task is active or recoverable.
  • Temporary files: previews, candidate renders, and delivery files are stored temporarily and removed after success, failure, cancellation, or recovery cleanup, subject to normal operating-system behavior.
  • Presets and LUTs: user-created presets and imported LUTs remain until you delete them or remove the App's local data.
  • Workspace data: completing or replacing a session removes related managed work files when they are no longer needed. Uninstalling the App removes its local container, subject to Apple's platform behavior.
  • Exports: successfully delivered videos remain wherever you chose to save them until you delete them.

5. Free-export allowance and iCloud Keychain

Free users currently receive 30 successful full-quality exports. To record delivered exports, the App stores a minimal, append-only event in the system Keychain. Each event contains a random export-job identifier, policy/schema information, an App-family identifier, and commit time. It does not contain the filename, file path, Photos asset identifier, video metadata, camera profile, LUT, editing recipe, or media content.

If iCloud Keychain is enabled, Apple may eventually synchronize these Keychain items among your eligible iPhone, iPad, and Mac devices signed in to the same Apple account. The App does not actively contact an account or quota server, cannot read your Apple ID, and cannot force or guarantee when Apple completes synchronization. Devices can therefore show temporarily different remaining counts while offline or before Keychain convergence. Reinstalling the App does not guarantee that the allowance resets.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are provided by Apple StoreKit. Apple processes payment credentials, billing, storefront pricing, taxes, subscription management, refunds, and purchase history under Apple's terms and privacy policy. The App receives verified product and entitlement information needed to display products and grant Pro access. We do not receive your card number or Apple account password.

7. Information we do not collect

  • No developer-operated user account or registration.
  • No advertising SDK, advertising identifier, or cross-app tracking.
  • No developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • No upload of source media, exported media, LUTs, presets, or editing recipes to us.
  • No sale, rental, or trade of personal information.

8. Apple services and third parties

The App relies on operating-system services including App Store/StoreKit, Photos, Files, iCloud Photos when applicable, and iCloud Keychain when enabled. Apple may process information necessary to provide those services under Apple's own policies. We do not operate an independent backend that receives that information.

9. Your controls

  • You choose which videos, files, and LUTs the App may access.
  • You can change Photos permission in system Settings, although original-quality import may stop working without the required access.
  • You can remove current tasks, presets, LUTs, and exported files using available App or system controls.
  • You can uninstall the App to remove its local container. Keychain records and files already exported outside the container may remain under Apple's platform behavior and your own storage choices.
  • You can manage subscriptions and purchases through your Apple account and the App Store.

10. Security

The App uses Apple's sandbox, system permission controls, StoreKit verification, and Keychain protection. No method of storage is completely secure, so you should keep important source media backed up and protect access to your devices and Apple account.

11. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not require a developer-operated account. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated service.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

3. 照片与文件访问

照片

您通过 Apple 的系统照片选择器选择视频。为了保留原始媒体质量和元数据，本应用可能请求照片图库读写权限，并使用您所选项目的标识，通过 Apple Photos 框架解析该项目的原始视频资源。本应用不会利用该权限浏览或上传您未选择的媒体。

如果所选原件只存在于 iCloud 照片中，Apple 可能将其下载到您的设备。该传输由 Apple 提供，并受您的 Apple 账户、设备设置和 Apple 隐私条款约束。

文件

您可以通过 Apple 的系统文件选择器选择视频和 LUT。本应用只访问您主动选择的项目，以及操作系统针对这些项目授予的安全作用域权限。

4. 本地存储与保留

  • 工作副本：导入的媒体会复制到应用管理的存储中，使所选源文件保持不变。当前任务处于活动或可恢复状态时，工作副本可能继续保留。
  • 临时文件：预览、候选渲染和交付文件会临时保存，并在成功、失败、取消或恢复清理后删除，但仍受操作系统正常行为影响。
  • 预设与 LUT：用户创建的预设和导入的 LUT 会保留到您主动删除或移除本应用本地数据。
  • 工作区数据：完成或替换会话后，本应用会在不再需要时清理相关工作文件。卸载应用会按照 Apple 平台行为移除本地应用容器。
  • 导出文件：成功交付的视频会保留在您选择的保存位置，直至您主动删除。

5. 免费导出额度与 iCloud 钥匙串

免费用户当前可成功导出 30 个完整质量文件。为记录已交付的导出，本应用会在系统钥匙串中保存一条最小化、仅追加的事件。每条事件只包含随机导出任务标识、策略/架构信息、应用家族标识和提交时间，不包含文件名、文件路径、Photos 资产标识、视频元数据、相机配置、LUT、编辑配方或媒体内容。

如果您开启了 iCloud 钥匙串，Apple 可能最终将这些钥匙串项目同步到使用同一 Apple 账户登录的合资格 iPhone、iPad 和 Mac。本应用不会主动连接账户或额度服务器，无法读取您的 Apple ID，也无法强制或保证 Apple 何时完成同步。因此，设备离线或钥匙串尚未收敛时，不同设备可能暂时显示不同的剩余额度。重新安装本应用并不保证免费额度会重置。
